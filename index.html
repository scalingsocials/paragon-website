Our Saga

Four decades of craft, one careful piece at a time

Founded in 1981, Paragon Metal Industries began with a team of skilled artisans and a shared passion for bringing Indian craftsmanship to the world.

From our roots in brass handicrafts, we have grown into a manufacturer and exporter of home décor, lighting and interior accessories. Our collections bring together traditional skills and contemporary design, with thoughtful attention to materials, finishes and the smallest details.

Over four decades, our journey has been shaped by the dedication of our people and the trust of our clients. A commitment to quality, careful packaging and timely delivery remains at the heart of every relationship we build.

Today, we continue to create with the same care and conviction that inspired our beginnings, honouring our craft while evolving with the homes and lifestyles of a changing world.

Rooted in Moradabad. Crafted by Paragon.

Where metal becomes beauty and purpose

Since 1981, Paragon’s story has been closely woven with the craft traditions of Moradabad. This heritage shapes who we are: the materials we work with, the skills we value and the care we bring to every collection.

Behind our pieces are skilled artisans whose knowledge and experience turn metal into objects of beauty and purpose. Their hands shape the forms, refine the textures and bring out the details that give Paragon’s creations their distinctive character.

  1. Selecting materialsMetals and accents chosen for how they will age, feel and finish.
  2. ShapingArtisans form each piece by hand, refining texture and line.
  3. AssemblingComponents come together with attention to balance and function.
  4. FinishingPolish, patina and plating bring out each piece’s character.
  5. Careful packingPacked to arrive as it left our floor, wherever it is headed.

“Quality is a responsibility shared at every stage, with equal care for the details our clients see and those they may never notice.”
